All Twenty-Five Alight

The tenth candle in the Left Corridor makes twenty-five in all. Nothing announces it. The dark just stops being where it was.

Beyond the last flame there is a way through that wasn't there before, into a corridor of hanging mirrors. It is open now, and I can Travel into it.

The candles have also left me a bargain. Feed your hours to the flames takes forty seconds, and my vitality drains faster while I feed them, but afterwards the rooms I know by heart go faster.
