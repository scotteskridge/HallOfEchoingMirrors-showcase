The Dark Beyond

The Left Corridor gives up on walls. Past the last frame the moulding stops, and the floor carries on into a dark so complete I can hear my own pulse.

I stand at the edge and tell myself my eyes are adjusting. They are not. I am being a coward for one more breath.

Then something in the dark catches my light, throws it back and turns away. A mirror, hanging on nothing. Another beyond it, and another.

*He came through here.* I cannot say how I know. I know it the way I know my own handwriting.

The way on is open: I can *Travel* from the Left Corridor to The Dark Corridor with Hanging Mirrors. There are candles to light here first, and I would rather go in with light.
