A Glimmer

A third of the way along the Right Corridor the frames crowd together into a wall, which is rude of them.

I have turned to go back when something at the edge of my eye stays put while the rest moves. A glint, low down by the skirting. It vanishes when I look straight at it and returns when I look away.

Something small is lying there that is not glass. In this place that is worth knowing about, and worth more than a guess from six feet off.

I can *Search* the Right Corridor now. It will cost me, but it might be something I can pick up and keep.
