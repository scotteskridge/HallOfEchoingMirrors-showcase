The Mana Stone

On the shelf above the bench, behind the jar of pumice where it always lived, sits Roland's reservoir stone.

He charged it a little every evening, the way other men bank a fire, so there would be something to draw on in the morning. I teased him about it for years.

In here it is full. I put both hands round it and drink, and I do not try to take it. Nothing leaves this room.

It goes into me like warmth into cold fingers. When I let go, the stone is dark, and it stays dark. Some things here are given once.

[PLACEHOLDER] I carry the warmth out with me. What it buys me, I will find out on the road.
