The Memory Is Wrong

The rain on the skylight is right. So is the smell of the spirit lamp, and the book lying open under it, and his notes spilling off the end of the bench.

He is right too. This is the Roland of that night, young and busy and pleased with himself, not the one I came in for. I have to keep telling myself the difference.

Then the small things add up. The lamp is on the wrong side. The bench is too clear, and there is a gap in it I cannot name, the way you know a tooth is missing before you find it with your tongue.

I have kept this night for twenty-one years, and I kept it better than this. Something has been taken out of it.

The Mirror's Laboratory now lets me *Watch him*, *Attend*, *Study the tome* and *Draw on the mana stone*. Watching him is the place to start.
