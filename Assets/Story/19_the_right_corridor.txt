The Right Corridor

The Right Corridor runs straight, and the frames down both walls each show a room I nearly know.

A parlour with our curtains and someone else's piano. A schoolroom with my handwriting on the board, spelling a word I have never used.

I stop looking after the sixth. This place is very good at almost, and I have work to do.

I can *Search* the Right Corridor. Anything worth carrying out of here will be small, and low down, and easy to walk past.
