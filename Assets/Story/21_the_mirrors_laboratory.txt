The Mirror's Laboratory

I step down from the glass onto our old floorboards, and the third one creaks. It always did. I had forgotten, and the room had not.

It is night, and raining. The lamps are lit. A man is at the bench with his back to me, and I know the back before I let myself know who it is.

He does not turn. This room finished happening twenty-one years ago, and nobody has told it I am here. Roland's ring has gone warm in my hand.

I can *Search* the Mirror's Laboratory. Whatever has been done to this night, I mean to find it.
