Mirrors in the Dark

Five mirrors now that I have gone near enough to see into properly. It takes the fifth for me to understand what I am looking at.

The nearest is my mother's scullery. The tin bath hangs on the wrong nail, where it hung for thirty years because nobody in that house ever thought to move it.

The next is a staircase I have never climbed, in a house I have never entered. A young man sits on the bottom step with his head in his hands. It is Roland, long before I knew him. There is no ring on his hand yet.

These mirrors show others' pasts, mine and his; people I've known and places I've never been to. Hung side by side in the dark. Somebody has been keeping them together, and nobody asked me.

Five more hang in the Dark Corridor. I want to see them all.
