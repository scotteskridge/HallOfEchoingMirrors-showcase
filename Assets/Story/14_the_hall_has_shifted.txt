The Hall Has Shifted

The Hall has moved while I was out. Every room I searched is unsearched again, as if I had never lifted a candlestick in it. I suppose I should be grateful it doesn't rearrange the furniture as well.

The ways are another matter. The doors I found stay found. A Dark Hall still leads to the Left Corridor and the Right, and I don't have to find them twice.

So each run starts the same way. I search again what I need to search, and I use the doors I already have. Travel gets me to a room, and Search gets me what is in it.
