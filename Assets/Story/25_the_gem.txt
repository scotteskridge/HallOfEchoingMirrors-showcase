The Gem

It holds.

Seven facets, cut true, and the last one catches the lamp and keeps it. It is a copy of the gem she took from my throat, and a good one. I would know the work for mine anywhere.

One facet, the amber, has a little warmth in it. I do not know yet what that means.

The Smoky Mirror I came in through will know this stone. I can *Exit through the glowing mirror* there, and go home.
