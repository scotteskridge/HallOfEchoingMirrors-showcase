The Chase Ends

I am not gaining on them. I have not been gaining for some while, and I keep running anyway, which is what twenty-one years of good sense comes to when a man's coat goes round a corner ahead of you.

The hall lengthens. I watch the same frame slide past twice, and there is more corridor ahead than when I started.

The gem goes cold one colour at a time. Amber first, then citrine, then the rest in an order I am too winded to mark. Each leaves like a lamp going out in a room behind me.

*Slow down. You are being eaten.*

I do not slow down. My legs give out past the ninth identical frame, and the floor comes up without any interest in whether I am ready.

She is standing over me before I finish falling. She has my face, exactly. She crouches and works the clasp of my chain with the same turn of the wrist I use. Of course she does.

*Hold on to it.* I cannot close my hand.

I wake with the laboratory in front of me, and I am not in it. There is the bench, the lamp with its cracked green shade, my own chair pushed back from my own desk. I am looking out through the glass from the side nobody is meant to stand on.

Behind me, Roland is struggling, and the thing in his coat has him by the arm, exactly where they were when I first came through. The hall has reset itself and set them walking again. I suspect it means to keep doing that.

I take four steps. My hand goes to my throat, as it has every day for twenty-one years, and finds only skin. I have nothing to spend, and that corridor lengthens faster than I can run it.

I stop and breathe and think of what I can do next.

I begin again, from the Smoky Mirror. I can Search it, and I can Gather a wisp when my vitality runs low.
