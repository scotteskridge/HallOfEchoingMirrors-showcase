The Other Laboratory

Ten mirrors, and the tenth will not turn. The rest drift; this one hangs square to me and shows our laboratory.

Not the one I left this morning, with the children's drawings pinned over the sink. The first one, from before there was a sink. Roland's notes cover the bench in the dreadful handwriting he had before I made him improve it. A book lies open between two lamps. I know which night this is from the angle of the chair.

I put my fingers to the glass, braced for cold. It gives like a door that was never locked, only waiting for someone to try it.

There is a night in that room I was never told about. The Dark Corridor with Hanging Mirrors now leads to The Mirror's Laboratory, and I can *Travel* there.
