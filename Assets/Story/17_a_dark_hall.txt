A Dark Hall

The gap lets me out into a hall with no lamps and no far wall that I can find. My footsteps come back to me a beat late.

It smells of tallow and cold stone, like a church after evensong. Someone has burnt candles here, a great many, and not lately.

Roland is not here. I did not expect him to be, and I look anyway.

The dark costs me while I stand in it; I can feel the bar going faster. I can *Search* A Dark Hall to see what it keeps.
