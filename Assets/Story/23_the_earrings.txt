The Earrings

The blue box is at the corner of the bench, where it always should have been.

That night I did not look at it once. I open it now. White sapphires, small and very good. He spent a term's salary on them and pretended he hadn't, and he had them attuned to me before he had worked out how to hand them over.

They are warm when I put them in.

They hold memory well; they were made to. I can *Draw a dense wisp* into them, two at most, and each is worth four of the corridor's wisps.
