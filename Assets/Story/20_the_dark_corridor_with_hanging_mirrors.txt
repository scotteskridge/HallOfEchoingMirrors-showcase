The Dark Corridor with Hanging Mirrors

My candles reach the first few yards and give up. Past that, the only light is what the mirrors catch and pass along to one another.

They hang on nothing, at every height, turning slowly as if in a draught. There is no draught.

Each one shows a room, but only for a moment before it turns its back. I will have to get close.

I can *Search* the Dark Corridor with Hanging Mirrors. Each search brings one more mirror near enough to see into properly.
