## start_gather_wisp
# Beginning to gather a wisp.

I hold out my hand and wait, the way one waits for a cat to decide.
There is a thread of light near the glass. I go after it slowly.
I cup my hands. The trick is not to want it too obviously.
A thread of light drifts past. I let it think it was its own idea.
A wisp drifts off the old glass. I keep very still and let it come.
Slowly now. A wisp shies away if I reach like I mean it.
I open my hand under the thread of light and wait for it to settle.
There is one by the frame's edge, thin as cotton. I go for it.
Twenty-one years, and I am back to catching light in my hands like a first-year.
I hold my breath. The wisp doesn't care, but I feel more careful.
The light hangs by the glass, deciding. I give it all the time it needs, and not a second more.
A thread of light, low by the skirting. I crouch for it.
I want this one badly, which is exactly how to lose it.


## gathering_wisps
# While gathering wisps.

The wisp is warm, the way a cup is warm after the tea has gone.
They drift towards whatever is still. I am learning to be still.
I pocket another. It hums against my hip like a small, polite bee.
Each one gives back a little of what the hall takes. A little.
I close my hand slowly. Too fast, and it slips away like steam.
This one is dimmer than the last. I take it anyway.
Each wisp is a small lost thing. I try not to wonder whose.
A wisp settles on my sleeve and stays, as if it had been waiting.
I hold very still. Somewhere, a wisp decides I am furniture.
They are easier to catch when I am not looking straight at them.


## start_instantiate
# Beginning to make something solid out of a mirror.

I put my hand to the glass and ask the reflection for the real thing.
I find the object in the mirror and do not let myself doubt it.
Roland would call this closure. I call it not taking no for an answer.
Dozens of frames on every wall, and only a handful with anything close enough to reach.
I choose my frame. Pick the wrong one and I am only a woman with her hand against a window.
The thing behind the glass is close enough. I reach in and insist.
I fix the shape in mind first: the weight, the edges, where the light sits on it.
Hand flat on the glass. The cold goes up my arm, and I keep it there.
This is the first thing I ever taught a student, and the hardest to do tired.
I look at the thing behind the glass until it starts to look like mine.
A reflection is only a promise. I am here to collect on one.
The glass resists, then thinks better of it.
I make myself certain. Doubt is what leaves you holding air.


## instantiating
# While instantiating (flint and steel, candles, the phial, the satchel).

The glass resists, then gives, like a drawer that sticks in damp weather.
It comes out cold and a little too perfect, as mirrored things do.
I hold the shape in my mind until my hand agrees with it.
Somewhere, someone is missing this. I try not to wonder who.
My arm is in the glass to the elbow. I do not think too hard about where my hand is.
The other mirrors go on showing their rooms. Only this one lets me in.
The glass closes round my wrist like cold water. I keep hold.
It slips once. I set my jaw and try again.
The thing in the glass has the right weight before it has the right shape.
My fingertips go numb first. They always do.
It comes out the way a splinter does: slowly, then all at once.
The reflection frowns at me as I take from it. I frown back.
For a moment I hold two of it, one on each side of the glass. Then only one.


## candlelight
# While Candlelight follows her (the candles are lit this run).

The candles drift after me like ducklings with opinions.
The dark gives ground, slowly, and makes it clear that it minds.
Fifteen small flames, and every one of them is on my side.
The dark backs off a foot and sulks there.
With the candles lit, I can breathe a little easier. Only a little.
The flames lean towards me when I stop, as if to ask why.
The light holds the dark off, and the dark takes less of me.
A candle gutters. I shield it with my hand until it thinks better of it.
I walk slower with the candles. There is no sense leaving them behind.
Wax on my cuff. I will deal with that at home.
In the candlelight, the nearest mirrors turn their faces away.


## satchel
# While she has the satchel this run.

Twenty-one years on, the leather still smells of Evercrest chalk.
Room for more. The hall, I suspect, has noticed.
Plenty of room. I intend to use all of it.
The satchel bumps my hip at every step, the way it did on the walk to lectures.
Everything I carry has a place now. I feel almost organised.
The strap finds the old groove in my shoulder as if it never left.
I check the buckle without thinking. Twenty-one years of habit.
There is ink on the lining from an examination I nearly failed.
My hands are free again. I had forgotten how much I like that.
It is heavier than it was this morning. That is the point of it.
I pack it the way Roland never does: heaviest at the bottom.
A tutor once called it a disgrace. It has outlasted her.


## hall_shifted
# Searching in a later run: the hall has moved things since last time.

I know this room. It no longer knows me.
Everything I found yesterday has been tidied away, like a lesson I was meant to learn twice.
The frames have been rehung. Somebody here has a great deal of time.
The frame I marked last time has moved three along. The mark has moved with it.
The door that was on my left is on my right. I did not turn round.
Different rooms in the glass. Same dust.
Everything I learnt here has been shuffled and dealt again.
I remember this room exactly, and it is wrong in five places.
Someone has been in here since. Someone always has.
