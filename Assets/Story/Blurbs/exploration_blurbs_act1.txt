# EXPLORATION BLURBS - ACT I
# Hall of Echoing Mirrors
#
# FORMAT
#   Lines beginning with # are comments and should be skipped by the parser.
#   Lines beginning with ## open a bucket. Everything after is that bucket's content
#   until the next ## line.
#   One blurb per line. Blank lines are ignored.
#
# USAGE
#   The script picks a BUCKET first, based on current game state, then a random
#   line from within it. Never pull at random across the whole file - a line about
#   the dark cannot fire before she has found the dark.
#
#   Do not repeat a line until the bucket is exhausted. Shuffle the bucket, walk it,
#   reshuffle. Pure random repeats within four or five pulls and the player notices.
#
# VOICE (rewritten 2026-09-29 against DesignNotes/Writing Guides/ProseVoiceGuide.md)
#   Clara at 41, first person, present tense. Twenty-one years after the book.
#   NOT frail - she is at the height of her practice and knows exactly what
#   everything costs. Dry where she was once earnest. Impatient rather than
#   frightened. She has a household and children waiting, and the hall is an
#   interruption she intends to survive. She does not explain the magic.
#   Every line is 20 words or fewer.
#
# COUNTS
#   Roughly 12-19 per major bucket. Aim for 30-40 each before ship so that a full
#   playthrough does not exhaust them. These are a starting set to write against.


## hall_corridor
# The mirrored corridor. Fires on traverse and search, loops 2-3, before the dark is found.

The frames go on. I have stopped counting them, which I suspect is what they want.
Every glass shows a room I almost know. The lamp is wrong. The lamp is always wrong.
My shoes make no sound on this floor. I find that rude.
Corridors narrow, or widen, or end. This one just goes on.
In the third frame a chair is pushed back from a desk. In the fourth, it is pushed back further.
One mirror shows a kitchen, a kettle boiling. It has been boiling for some time.
Dust, here. Dust means something settled, or something stopped.
The light comes from nowhere I can find. It is the colour of a room with the curtains drawn.
I keep catching movement at the edge of my eye and turning to find only my own coat.
Somewhere behind me a door closes. I did not pass a door.
The glass is cold when I touch it, and stays cold after I take my hand away.
Roland would have measured this and been happier for it.
Twenty-one years of practice and the first thing this place does is take it all off me.
The frames are hung at eye level. Somebody hung them. I do not care for that.
I have not been anybody's student in twenty-one years. The hall does not seem to have been told.
I left bread rising. I keep returning to that, which tells me something about my nerve.
I count seven steps between two frames, then eight. I stop counting.
Every frame shows a room somebody left in a hurry. I am beginning to take it personally.
The children will be home by four. The hall has not been told that either.
A hat stand stands in one frame. No hats. It looks embarrassed about that.
The clock in one frame says ten past four. So does the next. So does the next.
I tap the glass with a fingernail. It answers a moment late.
The floor is polished and nobody polishes it. I would like a word with the staff.
There is a draught, and no door for it to come from.
Roland always said a place this tidy was hiding something. He was usually right, and insufferable about it.
Someone has left a glove on a windowsill in the glass. Left hand, good leather. Not mine.
A frame shows a hallway very like my own. I do not slow down.
The corridor smells of beeswax and cold tea. A house well kept, and lately left.
Every mirror wants me to look at it. I look at the floor, which has manners.


## hall_dark
# The open dark with the floating mirrors. Unlocks after loop 2's discovery.

The walls have gone. The mirrors remain, turning on nothing, like leaves that forgot to fall.
There is no floor here and I am walking on it regardless.
Hundreds of mirrors, each looking out on somewhere that is not here.
A drawing room with the curtains shut against a summer that never ended.
A stair goes up into cloud. Nothing at the top of it that I can see.
One mirror shows a garden I planted. The roses are wrong by a season.
They all turn slowly, and none of them turn together.
I have begun to think of them as windows, which is a comfort and probably a mistake.
The dark is patient. I am not, and it knows.
Some of these are mine, some are his. I can tell which at a glance, and wish I couldn't.
One mirror holds me at twenty, very sure of herself. I do not look twice.
If I look too long at one, the others seem to lean.
No sound reaches here except my own breathing, and that only sometimes.
Somewhere in all this glass there is a way home. I am not looking for it yet.
I keep my eyes on the middle distance. The mirrors at the edges are the ones that look back.
I turn all the way round. Every direction is equally far from anywhere.
A mirror drifts past close enough to touch. I don't. It has the look of something that bites.
Rain runs down the inside of one frame. It is quite dry in here.
In one mirror the lamps are lit. In the next, they never were.
One shows a bakery I have never visited, and I am hungry regardless.
Far off, a single frame catches light and loses it. I mark where.
No draught, no smell, no edges. I would give a great deal for a wall.
I hold my own elbow for company and keep walking.
A mirror turns to show its back. Black, and no less watchful.
The near mirrors are dark and the far ones bright. I have decided that is a matter of temperature.
A mirror drifts by showing a street in snow. My breath does not fog.
I pick a bright frame and walk towards it. It keeps its distance.
The quiet here is heavy. I carry it like a wet coat.
One mirror shows a nursery, the cot empty and neatly made. I keep walking.
I say something aloud, just to hear it. The dark lets it go nowhere.
A child's shoe lies in one frame, laces undone. I make myself walk on.
In one mirror a table is laid for four. I only ever count three.


## searching
# Fires on search actions in any hall node.

Nothing. A frame, a wall, a length of floor that goes on being floor.
I run my hand along the moulding. Real wood. Somebody carved this.
There is a seam where two corridors were joined badly.
I find a coat button, brass and quite ordinary. I leave it.
The frames are not all the same size. I do not yet know if that matters.
Behind one glass, a room has been left in the middle of being tidied.
Something has been here before me and was not careful.
I search for a long time and find my own frown looking back.
There is a smell of cold iron, faint, and then not there at all.
The dust is thinner in a line down the middle of the hall. Somebody walks here.
I check behind the frame and under it. The frame is unimpressed.
I find a bent hairpin. Not mine. I put it back.
I lift the corner of a rug that isn't there. My hands have habits.
Nothing here. The nothing is very thorough.
I tap the moulding for a hollow. Solid all the way along, and smug about it.
A scrap of blue wool is caught on a splinter. I leave it hanging.
I check the skirting, the frame and the glass. Then the skirting again, out of spite.
My fingers come away clean. That is suspicious in a place this old.
Two scratches run side by side across the floor, where something was dragged. Long ago, or lately.
I hold my breath and listen. The Hall listens back.
I turn over a loose tile. Underneath, a smaller tile.
A chest of drawers stands in the glass. I reach for the top drawer and find only glass.
A pressed flower is wedged in the moulding. It crumbles when I touch it.
One frame hangs crooked. I straighten it. Nothing happens, but I feel better.
Under the frame there is a line of fine grey dust, like ash.
I find a silver thimble, worn through at the tip. I leave it on the frame.
The glass is scratched in one corner, as if someone tried to write something.
I press each panel in turn. The seventh gives slightly, then thinks better of it.
I search until my eyes ache and the frames start repeating themselves.
Something small rolls away from my foot and is gone before I see what.
A small wooden toy, worn smooth, in a frame that isn't ours. I put it back.


## ring_present
# The ring is visible at its node and she has not taken it. Loops 2-3.

A ring waits just behind the glass of one frame, close enough to touch. I know the setting.
I will have to drop something to make room in my pockets for this.
It is his. Of course it is his. The hall does not leave things by accident.
An empty ring, the stone gone dull. He would have said the word "uncharged" and meant nothing by it.
It has not moved since I last came this way. Nothing here moves unless it wants to be followed.
He took it off in my presence once, before he had any reason to. I know what that cost him.
A hundred committee meetings, and he turned that ring through every one of them.
The mirror is offering it to me. I have learned to ask what mirrors want in return.
It is the only thing in the Right Corridor that looks as though someone cared for it.
Taking it means paying for every step afterwards. I have done the sum twice.
Roland mislaid umbrellas by the dozen and never once his ring. Until now.
I stop at the frame again. The ring has not moved. Neither, I notice, have I.
Behind glass, like something in a museum case. He would hate that.
The claws stand open, waiting for a stone. I have no idea what he meant to put there.


## ring_carried
# While the ring is in hand. These should ache - it is taxing her. Say plainly that it costs her.

The ring weighs nothing, yet every step with it costs me.
I am tiring faster with it in my hand, and I know exactly why.
It is warm, which an empty stone has no business being.
The longer I hold it, the less of me there is to hold it with.
With it in my hand I feel him more clearly. That is precisely the trouble.
It wants something. It was built to want something.
Twice now I have caught myself turning it. He used to do that.
The hall is quieter while I hold it. I do not trust that.
Something had to leave my pockets to make room for it. I have stopped asking what.
He carried this for years and never once complained. I am beginning to resent that.
Every step with the ring is a step I pay for twice.
I could put it down and stop paying. I keep walking.
I have carried a sleeping child up three flights with less effort than this ring.
It grows heavier the further I take it. Not in the hand. Everywhere else.
The open claws catch on my sleeve. Even the ring is impatient.


## ring_refused
# After she has put it down. Fires occasionally in the corridor.

I left it where it lay. I could not spare what it was taking.
The ring is behind me somewhere. I have thought about it more than I would like.
I told myself it was only a ring. I have told myself worse.
It is only a ring. I will go back for it when I can afford it.
Roland would have told me to leave it. Roland would also have gone back for it.
Putting it down was the sensible thing. I have never liked the sensible thing.
My hand feels lighter, and so does the rest of me. I still look back.
I will need it later. I am almost sure I will need it later.
The drain eased the moment I set it down. So did my temper.
Twenty-one years married, and I have just left his ring on a floor.
I know exactly where it is. That is not the same as having it.
It will keep. The Hall keeps everything; that is rather the problem.


## low_vitality
# Fires when vitality drops below a threshold. Should feel like effort, not panic.

My hands have gone cold and are staying that way.
I am slower now. I notice it in the turns.
The tiredness is in my chest, not my legs. I felt it once before, beside the reservoir.
Something is being taken, and I can feel where from.
Not long now. I have learned roughly how long.
I have been emptied before, though never by anything that meant it.
I should turn back. I am aware that I should turn back.
The frames have begun to blur, and I do not think the fault is mine.
I would give a great deal for a hot cup of tea and a chair.
My vitality is running low. I can hear it, like a tap in another room.
My knees have started making decisions without me.
I lean on a frame for a moment. Only a moment.
I am running low, and the Hall is in no hurry at all.
My breath comes short on the easy stretches now.
I have a little left. I intend to spend it well.
My sight greys at the edges, the way it did after each confinement.
Every step costs a little more than the last. I am keeping count.
My hands are shaking. I put them in my pockets, where they are nobody's business.
When I blink, my eyes take a moment longer to open again.
I feel thin, like a letter held up to the light.
If I stop now, someone at home goes on waiting. I don't stop.
I promised them I would bring him back. I am rationing what is left.


## lab_sealed
# At the lab mirror before she can enter it. Loop 4 onward. She needs Roland's ring to get in.

The glass will not give. I have put my hand flat on it and felt it decline.
Through it: the long bench, the brass instruments, the lamp with the cracked green shade.
That is our laboratory. That is the night we cut the stone.
I can see the book open on the bench, and the two of us bent over it.
It is not a picture of that night. It is the night, kept here, and shut.
The other mirrors let me through. This one wants a key, and I think I know whose.
He is right there, young, with no idea what is coming.
Everything in that room is exactly where we left it. That should not comfort me.
The lamp in there is lit. Out here, the dark is not.
I knock on the glass, which is absurd, and nothing answers.
Every other mirror gives. This one keeps its door locked from the inside.
I stand at the glass until my palm aches with the cold. It does not change its mind.
Rain runs down the skylight in there. Out here there is no rain at all.


## reaching
# Muscle memory failing. She reaches for a hue that is not there.
# Fires early and often in loops 2-3, then TAPERS - by loop 4 she has mostly
# stopped reaching. Cut it off entirely once she has crafted the mirror gem;
# after that the loss is a fact rather than a discovery.

I reach for a colour without deciding to, and close my hand on nothing.
Twenty-one years. You would think the hand would learn.
I reached for amber again. Twenty-one years, and the hand still hasn't been told.
I go to read the room and find I am only looking at it.
A frame catches the light and I move to gather what comes off it. Nothing does.
The threads are there. I can see them. I simply cannot take hold.
It is like putting weight on a stair that is not where the stair was.
I keep opening my hand. I have started noticing that I keep opening my hand.
Something behind my ribs used to be full. I keep checking.
He asked me once what gathering felt like. I said: being handed something.
The girl I was would have panicked by now. I am merely furious, which is progress of a kind.
I know exactly which working opens this. I know all of it. I have nothing to pour in.
There is a name for this in the old texts and I have never needed it before.
I am rubbing my thumb across my fingertips, the way one does in cold.
