The Left Corridor

The Left Corridor is narrower than it looked from the hall. The frames hang so close their mouldings touch, and every glass shows this same corridor with the lamps out.

Between the frames there are sconces, iron, empty, one every few feet. Somebody meant this place to have light and then thought better of it.

I counted ten before the dark took the count off me.

I can *Search* the Left Corridor. If I bring candles and a way to light them, I can *Light a candle* in those sconces.
