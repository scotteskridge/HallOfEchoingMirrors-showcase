The Ring

It is in one of the mirrors along the Right Corridor, just behind the glass, exactly where the glint was, as if it had been set down there with care and not dropped.

A plain band, and a setting with nothing in it. The stone is gone, or was never put in; the claws stand open like a hand waiting to be given something.

I know it before I have reached for it. I have watched him turn it on his finger through a hundred dull committee meetings.

The glass would let me through. I can feel that from here: it is thinner around the ring, as if the mirror has been holding it out to me.

*It wants something. It was made to want something.*

I could bring it through. It will cost me; I can already feel it leaning on me, the way a small child leans when it is tired and has decided you are furniture.

Or I could leave it where it is, behind the glass, where it will be tomorrow, and the day after, and every day until I come back for it.

If I want it, I can *Instantiate Roland's ring*, and pay {cost per second} vitality for every step I carry it.
