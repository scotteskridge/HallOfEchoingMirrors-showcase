The Darkness Pushed Back

The fifteenth candle catches, and the room changes. The dark is still there, but it has stopped closing in on me. The light is holding it off, and I lose a quarter less vitality while it does.

I can see further now, though not far enough. There is a shape beyond the last candle that I can't make out.

The Left Corridor is dark too. Candles in there are where I'd try next.
