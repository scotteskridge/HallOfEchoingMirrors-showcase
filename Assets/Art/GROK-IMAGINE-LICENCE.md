## What the Terms say about generated images (section 4, "User Content")

"Output" means what the Service generates from your "Input" (prompt). Input plus Output is "User Content".

1. **Ownership.** "To the extent permitted by applicable law, and as between you and SpaceXAI, you retain your ownership rights to the User Content." Section 4 is headed "You Own Your User Content".
2. **Responsibility.** You are responsible for the User Content, and must have the rights to any Input you supply (for example, do not upload images you do not own).
3. **Attribution / brand.** "When using Output or SpaceXAI's name, logos, trademarks, or other brand elements, you are required to obtain our permission and attribute your generation of the Output to the Service, as detailed in our Brand Guidelines."
4. **AI disclosure.** You "may be required to apply" a disclosure that content was generated or altered by AI, or SpaceXAI may apply one.
5. **Not unique.** "Outputs may not be unique, and different users may receive similar Output"; your rights do not extend to others' rights in similar output.
6. **No warranty or indemnity from SpaceXAI.** Output is provided "as is". You indemnify SpaceXAI (section 11); SpaceXAI does not indemnify you if an image turns out to infringe someone else's rights.
7. **SpaceXAI's rights.** You grant SpaceXAI a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use Input you submit (section 4, "Our Use of User Content").

## Things to check for this project

- **Commercial use** is not explicitly named in the section above; ownership of Output is what allows it. The Enterprise Terms / API terms may differ from the consumer terms, so record which account type made each image.
- **Attribution and permission (item 3)** is the clause most relevant to a Steam release. It is unclear whether it covers ordinary use of Output inside a game, so check the Brand Guidelines (linked from the Terms page) and consider emailing support@x.ai for written confirmation. Keep any reply in this folder.
- **Steam** requires an AI-content disclosure on the store page.
- **Copyright:** in many countries, purely AI-generated images may have weak or no copyright protection, so "owning" them may not stop others copying them. This is general background, not legal advice.
- **Keep evidence:** save a dated PDF/screenshot of the Terms as they stood when the images were generated (the Terms can change), and note the generation date and account for each image.
