# Hall of Echoing Mirrors — Act II Design: the Amber Realm

The single reference for Act II, to read when Act I's content is finished and Act II is about to be planned and built. **Nothing here is built.** The plot is unwritten; every name, room and number is a placeholder (`~`) unless marked settled.

**Sources.** Gathered from the decisions-log entries of 2026-09-30 (from *Features come in layers* to *The classmate*). The log keeps the history and the reasons in full; this file is the current picture. When a new Act II decision is made, log it and update this file in the same session. Where this file and the GDD disagree, this file wins for Act II (the GDD sections it supersedes are listed at the end).

---

## 1. Shape of the act

- **One act per pool, in wheel order.** Act II is the Amber realm only: a cluster of about 5–7 nodes (`~`) off the Hall, drawn on the map like Act I's rooms.
- **It ends when Amber is in full use.** Its reward is **meta currency**, which arrives at the end of Act II and not before.
- **Act I before it:** 10–15 runs at minimum, 30 minutes to 2 hours (still being tuned).
- **Features come in layers** (GDD §14: no choices → one choice → a choice that persists). Act I: skills, mastery, switches, items, no stats. The end of Act I: walking out, stats, the empty Amber pool. Act II: Amber, binding, harvests. The end of Act II: currency.

## 2. Getting there: the end of Act I and the start of every Act II run

- **Act I ends** when she carries the cut gem back to the **Smoky Mirror** (the mirror she came in through) and walks out to the **real 1907 lab, which is the Hub.** She chooses to step back into the Hall each run, for Roland.
- **The gem** opens the exit mirror and gives the **Amber pool, empty.** No Amber magic is usable in Act I.
- **The first walk out wakes her stats** (the five stats start existing). **[PROPOSED, not settled]** They don't start at zero: the first exit gives a one-off starting amount, told as what the walk home did to her.
- **In Act II, stat XP settles at home.** A walk out keeps all of the run's stat XP; a collapse keeps part. **Placeholder rule:** a collapse keeps ~50%. Skill XP and mastery are always kept.
- **Every Act II run starts at the Smoky Mirror** and walks back through Act I's rooms, chasing Roland again: each act she gets closer. Mastery pays off and the old rooms keep a job. **Act I's rooms gain a few Amber spots** (see §4).

## 3. Pools: what Amber does

A pool has **three jobs**:
- **Shield:** each pool absorbs its own seventh of the drain while it holds pathos; an empty or missing pool's seventh falls on vitality. **Placeholder rule:** one-seventh each. With only Amber back, six-sevenths of the drain still hits vitality, so restoratives stay her main way to survive, and she grows visibly sturdier as each pool returns. **Travel charges always hit vitality directly.**
- **Fuel:** workings and binds spend it.
- **Threshold:** walls read how much she holds right now (magnitude: at least X; precision: inside a band).

Other pool rules:
- **Amber arrives empty** and **refills mid-run only by gathering** at Amber sources (not from wisps). Gathering takes time, so topping up always costs drain.
- **Its maximum rises only with meta currency**, so it is fixed through Act II.
- **Overcharge:** Amber can go above the maximum for one run only (the classmate's *Take*, §6). The real maximum doesn't move.

## 4. Where Amber comes from: only from others

- **Pathos is only ever gathered from others** (the novel's eromancy, kept). **Amber comes from people who are, or were, attracted to Clara.**
- In the realm: people in the memory (the classmate, §6; Roland at the heart, the deepest source).
- In Act I's rooms: **traces where Roland passed**, carrying his feeling for her. This ties gathering to the chase.
- Act I's wisps read as scraps of others' feeling the Hall has soaked up, so nothing built changes.
- Why it fits: GDD §12 *Memory nodes* already gives her transfer verbs against the memory's people (Listen, Console, Ask, Refuse, Take); Roland's core wound is guilt over teaching others to harvest eros (GDD §2); and *gather honestly or hold back* becomes a real choice.

## 5. The realm's rule: the memory pulls back

- **Inside a run:** anything she changes in a realm room (a moved ladder, a propped door, a shifted counterweight) **goes back to how the memory had it once she leaves that room.**
- **Across runs:** the realm has **no world switches.** It is whole at the start of every run.
- **An Amber bind is the only way to make a change stay put**, for the rest of the run or across runs.
- **Items are unchanged:** what she carries out through an exit is on her, not in the realm, so GDD §11 *Items persist by being carried out* still holds.
- Why: Amber is adhesion, and here the memory clings to how it was. The rule is felt every run, and the single bind is contested within a run as well as across runs.
- **UI need:** tooltips must say plainly what will slip back ("This will slip back when you leave").

## 6. Amber binding, harvests and the classmate

**Binding**
- Amber **holds things in place** in the realm and in Act I's rooms, across runs. (Walking out keeps what's on *her*; binding keeps things *where she left them*.)
- **Two dials:**
  - **Slots** (how many binds at once) are the puzzle rule. **She starts Act II with no slots**; the first harvest gives one. **A second slot can't be harvested until Act II is finished**, so grinding can't break the one-bind puzzle.
  - **Reservation** is the price: a bind spends some Amber once when made and holds back part of the Amber pool's maximum for as long as it stands, run after run.
- **Unbinding** is done at the bound thing, quick and free.
- **Binds can be puzzle objects or comfort.** With one slot, comfort has to be given up while the puzzle needs the bind. The first comfort bind is **a way held open** (a shortcut). Candidates still to choose: a pile held in place (a stash), a job kept done, a source held full.
- **Light isn't Amber's to bind.** Which hue owns light against the darkness is open: Citrine (joy; Act III), Amethyst (passion, fire) or Sapphire (concealment).

**The harvest (the soft cap)**
- Built on GDD §4 *Harvesting is an action with an escalating cost*: a high-cost action with a stat check, a cost that compounds with each repeat and eases back over runs, and the **next cost always shown**. The user wants a **large** increase per repeat (number open).
- A harvest takes a lot of feeling from someone in the memory and should feel heavier than a gather. Whom it takes from is open, with the plot.
- **In Act II, harvests pay only the first bind slot.** From the end of Act II, harvests also pay meta currency and further slots.

**The classmate** (placeholder): a boy in young Clara's class who had a crush on her she never noticed; the Hall shows her something she never knew. He is the realm's first **run-long choice**:
- ***Listen* (keep him):** while she is in the Lecture Room, Amber trickles in from him **while she does other actions there** (no queue slot of its own). It works only in that room and can't be carried out. Its use is getting things done there: the room's magnitude wall and fuel for binds. Because Amber shields only its seventh of the drain, it is a small help to survival, not a full restorative.
- ***Take* (burst him):** a large **run-only overcharge** of Amber, enough to pay for the first harvest and its slot. He turns away for the rest of the run, and the trickle goes with him.
- **The ordinary path:** Take him once to afford the first harvest and the slot; on later runs choose between the steady local trickle and an overcharge to carry elsewhere.
- **Watch in playtest:** whether Take dominates once the slot is earned. Expected to limit itself, because each later harvest costs far more than one burst gives.

## 7. The puzzle and the rooms

**The puzzle shape (the user's sketch).** Earn the slot (Take the classmate, harvest). Bind A to reach B; walk out with B. Unbind A and bind C to reach D. Then B and D plus binding E finish Act II. To keep it a puzzle rather than a checklist: show each object's need early (tooltips), and let the single bind compete with comfort binds.

**Plot frame (placeholder):** Clara's memory of meeting Roland, as his student. People in the memory see her as she was then (GDD §12 *Memory nodes*), so she has to play her younger self.

**Working room list (all placeholder: names, jobs and count open).** About a third are not memories (GDD §12 direction).

| # | Room | Kind | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Amber Threshold | Hall node | Entry from the Hall; a junction |
| 2 | The Lecture Room | Memory | The classmate (first Amber source, *Listen*/*Take*); the **magnitude wall** (Amber ≥ X) |
| 3 | The Sliding Stair | Constructed | **Bind A**, to reach… |
| 4 | Roland's Workshop | Memory | **Item B**, to walk out with |
| 5 | The Stopped Garden | Memory | A **Fork** |
| 6 | The Balance Room | Constructed | **Bind C**; the **precision wall** (Amber inside a band), leading to **D** |
| 7 | The First Meeting | Memory (heart) | Roland as the deepest source; **B + D + bind E** finish Act II |

**Walls:** a magnitude wall first, then a precision wall.

**Challenges in Act II:** Fork, run-long choices (the classmate is the first), Gamble, Trade-off pair; Gamble and the Trade-off pair go in rooms 4–6. **The permanent Ratchet is retired** (below). Sacrifice, Bargain and Pursuit wait for later acts.

**Why the Ratchet was retired:** a permanent loss works against the loop's "experiment freely"; players hoard it and never use it; it fails the GDD's own challenge tests (not informed the first time, and "spend it on the final push" dominates); the fixed Amber maximum caps its payoff; and it breaks the pull-back rule. Run-long choices and the escalating harvest replace it.

## 8. Story

- **What pulls against the rescue is the family in the real lab**, as story only (no mechanic yet).
- **Clara's reflection is a voice only** in Act II: collapse text and run-end lines, making its case. A body (a ghost runner, the Pursuit challenge) waits for Act III. Shadow stays tabled.
- **The narrative never holds the only copy of a puzzle clue:** skimmers must be able to play.
- Moral beats to write (`/writing`): taking the classmate's feeling (he never told her, and now she has taken it); harvesting in general (Roland's guilt).

## 10. UI and look needs (for `docs/UI-BACKLOG.md` when Act II is planned)

- The Amber bar's **overcharge** shown above the maximum (for example, a brighter section beyond the bar's end).
- **Reservation** shown on the Amber bar (the part held back by a standing bind).
- "Will slip back when you leave" on changes in realm rooms.
- The classmate's tooltip shows both choices from the first visit ("Taking all of it will lose him for this run").
- The harvest shows its current and next cost.
- Bound things marked on the map and in rooms, with the unbind action at each.
- **Colour as the payoff** (decisions log 2026-10-01): winning Amber colours only Amber's own things (its realm's rooms and lines, its pathos slot) and warms the map backdrop one step; the chrome stays gilt. The first time, the colour floods in over a few seconds; later runs start coloured. Act II shows Amber only (the *three hues* mock-up is Act IV's start). The hue Amber is a honey-orange kept apart from the gilt accent and the tooltip's "time" amber. Built in stage 2 with the Amber pool.
