What it shows

  1. Its own screen, the main screen's cousin. No top bar, no Story box: the map runs the full height and the queue column the full right edge. Same map, popovers, cards, fonts and palette, so nothing is built twice. It is a title, not a bar.
  2. By-heart progress on every room under its name: Known by heart (violet, with a dotted violet ring on the mirror), Worked here in N runs, by heart at 4, or Not worked yet. The room popover repeats it.
  3. The carried plan as one folded block per room ("A Dark Hall · by heart · 5 actions"). The fold button opens a block into its actions (see the third state). Player-added stops look as they do on the main screen. Drag to reorder, ✕ removes a block or an entry, Clear plan empties everything.
  4. Adding actions is the popover's +, as in play: appended after the last stop, with the walk added if the room is off the route. Ways and scheduling ahead work the same. ▶ is greyed because nothing is running.
  5. Room speed line under each room (numbers made up here; the game shows plan 025's real speed and the runs to its next step).
  6. Spare slot for a run-ahead strip, not chosen yet.
  7. Begin at the bottom of the queue: the only way out. The plan is saved, so quitting here keeps it.

Candles

Two candles at the outer edges of the map, each a flame that sways, a pool of warm light and a dark edge that breathes the other way, plus the mirrors' shadows leaning away from the nearest flame. Purely decoration: nothing in the simulation depends on it.
