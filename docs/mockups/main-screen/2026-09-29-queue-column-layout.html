What changes

  1. The queue is a column between the map and the Story, top to bottom, one card per stop (the room, the walk there, then its actions). The action she's doing now is at the top with its progress bar. Drag rows or whole stops up and down; ✕ removes. The header gives the count and the time left.
  2. Fold button under the queue. Folded, the column becomes a slim strip under the Story box (current action, its bar, what's next) and the map widens by 360 px. Open again, it pushes the map left, not over it. The game remembers the choice.
  3. Schedule works in any room she can reach. A room already on her route (here stop 3): the action goes in at the end of that visit and the stop lights up in the queue. A room off her route: the queue adds the walk there by the shortest way, then the action (switch the popover above to see its note). Only actions she truly can't do are greyed, with the reason. Play still acts only where she is now.
  4. Stop numbers on the map match the queue's cards, and her route is a dashed gold line. Optional: it ties the column to the map at a glance.
  5. No bottom strip. The full-width "Nothing queued" ribbon and the big Queue button are gone: the map runs to the bottom edge. With nothing queued, the column shows the hint instead.
  6. The Story box is narrower (about 400 px instead of about 480), so the queue fits beside it. Its cards keep their text size.
  7. Map size: with the queue open, the map gets about 60% of the width under the top bar (about 45% today, with a slab under it). Folded, about 80%.

Not in this mock-up

The slim top bar, backdrop and one material (plan ui-014), the tooltip redesign and the Button/Chip prefabs. The popover beside its node is shown because the columns move the map and the popover must follow it.
